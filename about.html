About EasyPDFPlay

Your documents are nobody’s business but yours.

We build simple, fast PDF tools that run entirely in your browser, so you can edit, sign and send paperwork without handing it to anyone.

Why we built EasyPDFPlay

Fixing a typo in a PDF or signing a form shouldn’t mean creating an account, starting a trial, or uploading a contract, ID or tax form to a stranger’s server. Yet most online tools ask for all three.

We thought there should be a better default: a tool that does the job, respects your privacy and gets out of the way.

What makes it different

Everything happens on your device. Your PDF is opened, edited and saved in your browser, and it never touches our servers.

Edits look right, too. EasyPDFPlay reads the fonts embedded in your PDF and reuses them, so changed text blends in with the original.

What we believe

Principles we build by

Private by default

Your documents are processed on your device. We designed the product so we never need to see them.

Simple by design

A clean, fast editor that makes sense the first time you open it. No tutorials, no clutter.

Fair and transparent

Free to use with no watermark, no dark patterns and no surprise paywalls. We tell you plainly what the tool can and can't do.

Built on open web technology

EasyPDFPlay stands on the shoulders of excellent open-source projects, running entirely in the browser.
